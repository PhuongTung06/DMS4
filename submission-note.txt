Cookie Kitchen — Project Note

Idea: Cookie Kitchen is a playful browser drawing game where users sketch or upload a cookie, bake it into a simple 3D treat, arrange it on a plate, resize it, and decorate it with piped frosting and 3D sprinkles.

Who it is for: People who enjoy casual creative tools, especially artists and users looking for a low-pressure way to play with drawing and decorating ideas.

What I explored: I combined pointer-based canvas drawing and image upload with transparent-pixel silhouette extraction, generated 3D cookie geometry and textures, drag-to-move and drag-to-resize interactions, adjustable raised frosting, 3D sprinkles, custom cursor states, undo controls and PNG export. Three.js supports the 3D scene (Three.js n.d.), and the platter uses a CC0 model by Innerscene Corp (n.d.). The interface fonts are served by Google Fonts (Google n.d.).

What I would improve: I would refine cookie conversion for complex outlines, improve touch controls on small screens, and let users save and reopen editable scenes in addition to exporting an image.

AI acknowledgement: I used ChatGPT (OpenAI 2026) to help write, debug and revise code for this project. I reviewed and adapted the suggestions.
